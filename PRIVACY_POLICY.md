# Privacy Policy for WikiCraft

Last updated: November 13, 2026

WikiCraft ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a lightweight browser extension that provides a clean, modern reading theme for Wikipedia (`*.wikipedia.org`).

We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how WikiCraft handles user information.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Usage

**WikiCraft does not collect, store, track, or share any personal information or browsing activity.**

- **No Personal Data**: We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, location data, or device identifiers.
- **No Browsing History**: We do not track or record the Wikipedia articles or web pages you visit.
- **No Analytics / Telemetry**: WikiCraft does not include any third-party tracking scripts, analytics tools, or advertising libraries.

---

## 2. Local Storage Usage

WikiCraft uses the browser's local storage API (`chrome.storage.sync` / `browser.storage.sync` / `localStorage`) solely to store your visual theme preferences:

- Whether WikiCraft is enabled or paused (`enabled`)
- Your selected appearance mode (`light`, `dark`, or `system`)

This configuration data is stored locally within your browser and is never transmitted to external servers or third parties.

---

## 3. Network Communications

WikiCraft runs entirely offline on your device within the context of `*.wikipedia.org` pages. It does not initiate any external network requests or transmit any data across the internet.

---

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published in this repository with an updated date.
